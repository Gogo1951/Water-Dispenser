# Water Dispenser // Test Plan

Water Dispenser fills the trade window with the water, food, healthstones and other items you set up to give. This time we most want you to try the reworded `- Dispenser` macro message, especially on WoW Forever, and the conjure buttons when other windows open beside a trade.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Click the `- Dispenser` macro in a party: the message should start with the square mark and end with "Water Dispenser", on WoW Forever too, and the preview under **Announcements** should match it.
2. As a mage or warlock, open your spellbook while a trade is open: the conjure buttons should stay right under **Fill Trade Window**.
3. Open a trade straight after logging in: the line under **Fill Trade Window** may say Loading for a moment, then should name every item.
4. If you copied your settings over from another game version, type `/wd` and open **Profiles**: characters from the other version should be gone.

## Everything Else

5. Trade a stranger, a party member and a raid member: each should get the right items, with 40 water in a raid instead of 20.
6. Trade a warrior or rogue, then a much lower-level player: the warrior or rogue gets food but no water, and the low-level player gets water they can drink.
7. As a mage or warlock, click a conjure button under **Fill Trade Window**: it should make a rank your partner can use and drop it straight into the trade.
8. Open a trade with Dispense on, then off (Left-Click the mini-map button): the line under **Fill Trade Window** should say what went in, then that Dispense is off.
9. As master looter in a dungeon or raid, open a trade: it should stay empty until you click **Fill Trade Window**.
10. Hover a group member who runs the add-on: their tooltip should list what they have to give, and a warlock's should show their Improved Healthstone rank.
11. Click the `- Dispenser` macro solo and in a raid: it should post your leftovers to Say or Raid with clickable item links.
12. Type `/wd`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, first out of combat and then in combat: the options should open only out of combat.
13. On WoW Forever, fight something while hovering group members and conjuring: no errors, and the tooltips come back once the fight ends.
