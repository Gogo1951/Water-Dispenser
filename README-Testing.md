# Water Dispenser // Test Plan

Water Dispenser fills the trade window with the water, food, healthstones and other items you set up to give. This time we most want you to try the new **Feedback & Support** page and the top-rank water on Season of Discovery.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Type `/wd` and open **Feedback & Support**: the links should be there to copy, with the version number at the bottom.
2. On Season of Discovery, as a mage of level 55 or higher, trade a player of level 55 or higher: they should get Mountain Spring Water.
3. With a trade open, hover the mini-map button, then conjure water: the new water should still go straight into the window.
4. On WoW Forever, log in alongside any add-on that restyles mini-map buttons: no errors, and the Water Dispenser button still shows.

## Everything Else

5. Trade a stranger, a party member and a raid member: each should get the right items, with 40 water in a raid instead of 20.
6. Trade a warrior or rogue, then a much lower-level player: the warrior or rogue gets food but no water, and the low-level player gets water they can drink.
7. As a mage or warlock, click a conjure button under **Fill Trade Window**: it should make a rank your partner can use and drop it straight into the trade.
8. Open a trade with **Enable Dispense** on, then off: the line under **Fill Trade Window** should say what went in, then that Dispense is off.
9. As master looter in a dungeon or raid, open a trade: it should stay empty until you click **Fill Trade Window**.
10. Hover a group member who runs the add-on: their tooltip should list what they have to give, and a warlock's should show their Improved Healthstone rank.
11. Click the `- Dispenser` macro: it should post your leftovers to Say, Party or Raid with clickable item links.
12. Type `/wd`, then Shift + Middle-Click the mini-map button, first out of combat and then in combat: the options should open only out of combat.
13. On WoW Forever, fight something while hovering group members and casting spells: no errors, and the tooltips come back once the fight ends.
